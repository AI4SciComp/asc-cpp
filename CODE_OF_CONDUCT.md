# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

We are committed to a welcoming, safe, and constructive project for everyone,
regardless of background, identity, experience, or role. Scientific and
technical disagreement is welcome; harassment, intimidation, and personal
attacks are not.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful, specific, and evidence-oriented.
- Critique ideas and results, not people.
- Credit prior work and disclose provenance.
- Respect privacy and avoid publishing another person's private information.
- Accept maintainer decisions gracefully and use the documented appeal path.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, discriminatory language, threats, sexualized attention, deliberate
disruption, doxxing, and retaliation are prohibited in all project spaces.

## Enforcement

Report conduct concerns privately through the security/contact route in
[`SECURITY.md`](SECURITY.md). Release approvers may remove content, restrict
participation, or ban a participant when necessary. Reports are handled as
confidentially as practical. A person named in a report will not adjudicate it.

This policy applies to repository discussions, reviews, project events, and
public representation of the project.
